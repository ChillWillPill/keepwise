Prices outside the United States

The website shows the price for your country before you pay, and the checkout shows the final amount with any local tax.

How paying works

  • Free trial: your first Plus purchase starts with 7 days free. Cancel before it ends and you pay nothing.
  • Billing: after the trial, Plus renews every month or every year until you cancel.
  • Cancel any time: from the link in your receipt email or your Paddle account. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds: a full refund within 14 days of your first payment or a yearly renewal, no reason needed. See the Refund Policy.
  • Who sells it: on the website, our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and handles payment, receipts, taxes and refunds.
