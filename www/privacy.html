1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use KeepWise, including the website at mykeepwise.com, the installable web app and the Android app (together, the "Service"). In this policy, "KeepWise", "we", "us" and "our" refer to the operator of the Service, and "you" refers to any person who uses it.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Use.

2. How the Service works

The Service has two separate parts.

  • Your financial information stays on your device. Calculations, budgeting, subscription detection, statement analysis and the answers given by the "Ask KeepWise" feature are performed by code running on your device. The information you enter or import for these features is saved in your browser's local storage or, in the Android app, in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us, and we have no means of viewing, accessing or recovering it.
  • Your account is stored online. If you create an account, the profile details described in section 5 are stored with our service provider, Google Firebase, so that you can sign in and so that we can provide account features. Your financial information is not part of your account.

3. Financial information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, the following information may be stored on your device only:

  • income, pay dates, budget categories, savings goals and amounts set aside;
  • other income you add, such as a side job, creator income, trading or rental income, including when you expect it, the payments you log and what you confirm you kept at the end of a month;
  • subscriptions, bills and expenses, including names, amounts, renewal dates and your notes on usage;
  • day-to-day spending you log by typing or speaking, including the name, the amount, the date and, if you paid in another currency, the original amount and the rate used;
  • trips you plan, including the name, dates, any budget, each amount you log against the trip and whether you chose to count it in a month or keep it separate. What you type to log spending is read on your device and is not sent anywhere;
  • loans, credit cards and other debts you add, including the type, a name you give it, the payment, the amount and the number of installments left, the end date and any yearly card fee. KeepWise never asks for a card number or an account number, and you should not enter one;
  • promotional codes and cheaper alternatives you save;
  • names of people you split costs with, and the amounts owed between you, and any groups you save;
  • if you choose to let KeepWise search your contacts, the names, phone numbers and email addresses of those contacts, as described in section 8;
  • a summary of any bank statement you import, as described in section 4;
  • monthly statements that KeepWise writes from your plan, one for each month, showing what came in, what went out, what you kept, your subscriptions, the cheaper swaps you used, the amounts you set aside and a count of your splits and settled payments;
  • the dates on which your subscriptions renew, if you add them or they are found in a statement you import;
  • your settings, such as currency, theme and notification preferences, and the date of your last backup.

Monthly statements

KeepWise writes each monthly statement on your device from the numbers already in your plan. Statements are not sent to us. If you choose to save a statement as a PDF or as a file, the file is created by your device and saved wherever you choose. We never receive a copy.

Backup files

You can download a backup file from the Plan tab. The file contains all of the information listed in this section and is created on your device. It is not sent to us. Anyone who has the file can read the information in it, so keep it somewhere safe and do not share it with people you do not trust. If you restore a backup, the information on your device is replaced with the contents of the file.

Moving your data to the Home Screen app

On iPhone, the KeepWise you add to your Home Screen keeps its information separately from your browser. To carry your information across, you can tap Copy my data in the browser and Paste my data in the Home Screen app. This places a copy of your KeepWise information on your phone's clipboard. Nothing is sent to us. The clipboard belongs to your phone, so anything you paste it into can read it. Paste it only into KeepWise, and copy something else afterwards if you want to clear it.

Messages to people in a split

When you add, change, delete or settle a split, KeepWise can write a short message for the people in it, such as their share of the bill. The message is written on your device. KeepWise does not send it. You choose whether to send it, and you send it yourself from an app on your phone, such as your messaging app. KeepWise does not see who you send it to or whether you sent it.

Images you share

The "Share what you found" feature creates an image on your device showing a single monthly amount, such as the money you saved by dropping subscriptions. It does not include your name, your income or the list of what you pay for. The image is shared only if you choose to share or save it, and only with the app or person you select.

This information remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by selecting "Start empty" in the Plan tab, by clearing the website data for KeepWise in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app. Deleting your account does not delete this information, and deleting this information does not delete your account.

If you turn on the app lock, KeepWise stores a protected, one-way form of your passcode on your device so it can check what you type. The passcode itself is never stored and never sent to us, and we cannot recover it. If you also turn on Face ID or fingerprint unlock, the check is carried out by your phone. KeepWise never receives your face or fingerprint data, only whether the check passed. The app lock controls who can open KeepWise on your device. It does not encrypt the information stored there.

If you log spending in a currency other than your own, your device asks a public exchange rate service for that day's rates so the amount can be converted. The request contains the currency and nothing about you or what you spent. As with any request over the internet, the service can see your IP address. The services used are the open currency data published through the jsDelivr content delivery network and, if that cannot be reached, open.er-api.com. Rates are indicative and may differ from the rate your bank applied.

Where your browser offers it, you can speak instead of typing. Your browser or phone turns the speech into text using its own speech service, which may process the audio on the provider's servers under that provider's terms. KeepWise receives only the resulting words, does not record or keep audio, and reads the words on your device.

4. Bank statements

When you import a bank statement in CSV or PDF format, the file is opened and read on your device. The file is not uploaded to us or to any other party. KeepWise extracts the transactions needed to identify your income, recurring payments, subscriptions and spending categories, and saves the results of that analysis on your device. The original file is not retained by the Service.

If your bank has protected the PDF with a password, KeepWise asks you for it. The password is used once on your device to open the file. It is not saved and it is not sent to us or to anyone else.

To read PDF files, your device downloads a standard open source PDF reading library, pdf.js, from the cdnjs content delivery network the first time you import a PDF. Only the library is downloaded. The contents of your statement are never sent to cdnjs or to any other party.

8. Contacts and notifications

Contacts

You can choose to let KeepWise search your contacts, so that you can find friends quickly when you add people to a split. Depending on your device, you either allow the Android app to read your contacts, choose contacts in your phone's contact picker, or select a contacts file (vCard). KeepWise asks before it reads anything.

KeepWise keeps only the name, phone numbers and email addresses of each contact, and keeps them on your device only, in the same storage as your other KeepWise information. They are used only to show matches when you search for people in a split. Your contacts are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and they are not part of your account. A contact is added to your People list only when you add that person to a split. You can remove all contacts from KeepWise at any time by tapping Remove next to your contacts on the Split tab, or by withdrawing the contacts permission in your phone's settings.

Groups you save, such as a group of friends you split with often, are also stored only on your device.

Notifications

In the Android app you can turn on reminders about your subscriptions. In the 10 days before your payday, or before a new month if you have not set a payday, KeepWise can show one notification a day that asks how much you use a subscription, with buttons to answer. The reminders stop for that month when you answer one or open the Subs tab, and they become less frequent if you do not respond.

With reminders on, KeepWise can also ask about a subscription about a week before its own renewal date, and can remind you once, the morning after your pay or other income was due, if you have not logged it. On every device, the Month tab may ask whether money that was due has arrived. If you give a bill a due day, KeepWise can remind you the day before it is due and ask once, from the day after, whether you paid it, and it does not ask again until the next due day. Your answers are stored on your device only.

Reminders are off until you turn them on, and your phone asks for your permission first. They are scheduled by the app on your device, using the subscriptions you entered. No push notification service, server or device identifier is used to deliver them, and we do not learn whether you received, opened or answered one. You can turn reminders off at any time on the Subs tab or in your phone's settings.
