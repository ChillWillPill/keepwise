2. How the Service works

The Service has two separate parts.

  • Your financial information stays on your device. If you use KeepWise Plus to connect a bank, read sales from Stripe or PayPal, or email the people in a split, the information that feature needs passes through the KeepWise server on its way, as described in section 8; it is not stored there. Calculations, budgeting, subscription detection, statement analysis and the answers given by the "Ask KeepWise" feature are performed by code running on your device. The information you enter or import for these features is saved in your browser's local storage or, in the Android app, in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us, and we have no means of viewing, accessing or recovering it.
  • Your account is stored online. If you create an account, the profile details described in section 5 are stored with our service provider, Google Firebase, so that you can sign in and so that we can provide account features. Your financial information is not part of your account.

Monthly statements

Messages to people in a split

Images you share

This information remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by selecting "Start empty" in the menu, under Your data & backups, by clearing the website data for KeepWise in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app. Deleting your account does not delete this information, and deleting this information does not delete your account.

If you turn on the app lock, KeepWise stores a protected, one-way form of your passcode on your device so it can check what you type. The passcode itself is never stored and never sent to us, and we cannot recover it. It does not upload your money information. To make this possible, KeepWise keeps the identifier and email address of the signed-in account on your device, and removes them when you sign out. Where your browser offers it, you can speak instead of typing. KeepWise receives only the resulting words, does not record or keep audio, and reads the words on your device.

4. Bank statements

When you import a bank statement as a CSV file, a PDF, a scanned PDF or a photo, the file is opened and read on your device. The file is not uploaded to us or to any other party. KeepWise extracts the transactions needed to identify your income, recurring payments, subscriptions and spending categories, and saves the results of that analysis on your device. The original file is not retained by the Service.

Before anything is added, KeepWise shows you each transaction as your bank wrote it, with its date and amount, and where it has been placed. Only the lines you tick are added to your plan, and only when you choose to add them. If you type the name your bank uses for you, so that transfers between your own accounts can be set apart, that name is used on your device for that purpose only and is not sent to us.

If your bank has protected the PDF with a password, KeepWise asks you for it. The password is used once on your device to open the file. It is not saved and it is not sent to us or to anyone else.

To read PDF files, your device downloads a standard open source PDF reading library, pdf.js, from the cdnjs content delivery network the first time you import a PDF. Only the library is downloaded. The contents of your statement are never sent to cdnjs or to any other party.

A scanned statement or a photo is a picture, so the words in it have to be read from the image. To do this, your device downloads a standard open source text recognition library, Tesseract, from the jsDelivr content delivery network the first time you import a scan or a photo. Only the library is downloaded. The reading happens on your device, and the picture and its contents are never sent to jsDelivr, to us or to any other party. KeepWise does not keep the picture.

8. Contacts and notifications

Contacts

You can choose to let KeepWise search your contacts, so that you can find friends quickly when you add people to a split. Depending on your device, you either allow the Android app to read your contacts, choose contacts in your phone's contact picker, or select a contacts file (vCard). KeepWise asks before it reads anything.

KeepWise keeps only the name, phone numbers and email addresses of each contact, and keeps them on your device only, in the same storage as your other KeepWise information. They are used only to show matches when you search for people in a split. Your contacts are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and they are not part of your account. A contact is added to your People list only when you add that person to a split. You can remove all contacts from KeepWise at any time by tapping Remove next to your contacts on the Split tab, or by withdrawing the contacts permission in your phone's settings.

Groups you save, such as a group of friends you split with often, are also stored only on your device.

Notifications

In the Android app you can turn on reminders about your subscriptions. In the 10 days before your payday, or before a new month if you have not set a payday, KeepWise can show one notification a day that asks how much you use a subscription, with buttons to answer. The reminders stop for that month when you answer one or open the Subs tab, and they become less frequent if you do not respond.

With reminders on, KeepWise can also ask about a subscription about a week before its own renewal date, and can remind you once, the morning after your pay or other income was due, if you have not logged it. On every device, the Month tab may ask whether money that was due has arrived. If you give a bill a due day, KeepWise can remind you the day before it is due and ask once, from the day after, whether you paid it, and it does not ask again until the next due day. Things you pay once a year, such as yearly giving or a card's yearly fee, are handled the same way around their date each year. Your answers are stored on your device only.

Reminders are off until you turn them on, and your phone asks for your permission first. They are scheduled by the app on your device, using the subscriptions you entered. No push notification service, server or device identifier is used to deliver them, and we do not learn whether you received, opened or answered one. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings or in your phone's settings.

Notifications in the web app

In the web app, including on iPhone after you add KeepWise to your Home Screen, you can turn on notifications in Settings. KeepWise then sends you one short nudge two days before your payday, or before a new month if you have not set a payday. The nudge is the same for everyone and says nothing about your money.

To deliver it, your device works out the dates of your next few nudges and sends only those dates, your time zone and a push address created by your browser to our database, run by Google Firebase. Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers the nudge. No names, amounts, bills, subscriptions or account details are sent. The dates are updated when you open the app and your pay schedule has changed.

Notifications are off until you turn them on, and your browser or phone asks for your permission first. When you turn them off in Settings, the push address and dates are deleted. If your browser stops accepting notifications, we delete them the next time a nudge fails to arrive.

Connecting your bank (United States)

You can choose to connect a US bank through SimpleFIN Bridge, a service run by SimpleFIN that links to banks and credit unions. You sign up with SimpleFIN yourself and pay them directly; KeepWise is not paid and is not part of that agreement. When you paste the setup token SimpleFIN gives you, your device exchanges it with SimpleFIN for an access link and stores that link only on your device, under a separate key that is never included in backups or in "Copy my data". Your device then asks SimpleFIN for your accounts, balances and the last 90 days of transactions, directly, with no KeepWise server in between. KeepWise never receives your bank data, your bank sign-in or the access link. Account names and balances are kept with the rest of your data on your device; transactions are read only when you review an account, and you choose which of them to add. Disconnecting removes the access link from your device; to end the connection completely, also remove KeepWise in your SimpleFIN account.

KeepWise Plus: connecting a bank through Plaid (United States)

If you use KeepWise Plus, you can connect a US bank through Plaid Inc. instead. You sign in to your bank inside Plaid's own window, which KeepWise cannot see; your bank sign-in goes only to Plaid and your bank. Plaid then sends your account names, the last four digits of each account number, balances and up to 90 days of transactions. Plaid handles your information under the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

These requests pass through the KeepWise server, a small program run for us by Cloudflare, because Plaid requires a server to hold KeepWise's Plaid keys. The server keeps no database and does not log what passes through it. The access token Plaid issues for your bank is sealed by the server with encryption only it can undo, and only the sealed version is given to your device, which sends it back each time you refresh. Your transactions, accounts and the sealed token are stored only on your device, under a separate key that is never included in backups or in "Copy my data", and only the last 90 days of transactions are kept. Transactions are read into KeepWise only when you review an account, and you choose which to add. Disconnecting asks Plaid to end the connection and removes everything about it from your device.

KeepWise Plus: live sales from Stripe and PayPal

If you use the Business space in KeepWise Plus, you can connect your own Stripe or PayPal account so your sales come in by themselves. For Stripe you create a restricted key that can only read; for PayPal you create your own app and copy its Client ID and Secret. These keys are stored only on your device, under a separate key that is never included in backups. Each sync sends the key and the dates to read through the KeepWise server to Stripe or PayPal, and hands the answer straight back; the server does not store or log the keys or your sales. KeepWise only reads: it cannot charge, refund or move money. From each sale KeepWise keeps the date, the description or the payer's name, the amount and the fee, as business entries on your device. Disconnecting removes the key from your device; to end access completely, also delete the key or app in your Stripe or PayPal account.

KeepWise Plus: emailing the people in a split

If you use KeepWise Plus and are signed in, KeepWise emails the people in a split their part of it when you add, change, delete or settle a split, and, in the first week of each month, emails you (only you, at your account email) a summary of who owes whom. Only people you have saved an email address for are emailed, and you can turn automatic emails off at any time on the Tell them card, or send them yourself with a tap. For each person emailed, the KeepWise server passes their first name, the email address you saved for them, the split's title and the short message shown on your screen to Resend, the service that delivers the email. The email comes from splits@notify.mykeepwise.com, shows your first name, and replies go to your account's email address. KeepWise does not keep these addresses or messages on its server and sends the people in your splits nothing else. Anyone who doesn't want these emails can reply and ask you to remove their address. The email addresses you save for people stay with your other data on your device.

KeepWise Plus: emailing invoices to your clients

If you add a client's email address to an invoice and are signed in to KeepWise Plus, you can have KeepWise email them the invoice. If it becomes late, KeepWise sends the client a polite reminder 3 days and again 10 days after the due date, unless you turn reminders off. For each email, the KeepWise server passes the client's name and email address, your business name, the message, the invoice picture and your account email (for replies) to Resend, which delivers it from invoices@notify.mykeepwise.com. Nothing is kept on the KeepWise server. Before adding a client's email, make sure they expect invoices from you.

KeepWise Plus: your logo from your website

In the Business space you can ask KeepWise to find your logo on your website. Only the website address you entered is sent to the KeepWise server, which opens that site's home page, finds its icon or share image and hands the picture back to your device. Nothing is kept on the server. The logo is then stored with your business profile on your device. If you add a PayPal.me, Venmo or Cash App username, your invoices carry a payment link to that service; your clients open it on that service's own website.

Finding a place on the map

When you plan a trip or an event you can add where it is. If you type at least three letters in that box, the words you type are sent to Photon, a free map search run by komoot that uses OpenStreetMap data, so it can suggest matching places. Nothing else is sent: not your name, your amounts, your dates or anything else in KeepWise. If you pick a suggestion, its name and map position are saved with the trip on your device. You can also leave the box empty, or keep the words you typed without picking a suggestion. Tapping a saved place opens it on the OpenStreetMap website.
