Refund Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

KeepWise is free to use. This policy covers KeepWise Plus, the optional paid plan. Plus is not on sale yet; this policy applies from the day it is.

1. Who sells Plus

On the website, Plus is sold by our reseller and merchant of record, Paddle.com. Paddle handles the payment, the receipt, taxes and refunds. On Android, Plus is sold through Google Play and follows Google Play's refund rules. On iPhone, if Plus is offered through the App Store, Apple's refund rules apply.

2. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. Plus stays on until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again. Cancel from the link in your receipt email, from your Paddle account, or from your Google Play subscriptions.

3. Refunds

If Plus is not right for you, ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment or of a yearly renewal. No reason is needed. After 14 days, payments are not refunded, except where the law says otherwise. If a technical problem on our side stops Plus from working and we cannot fix it, we refund the affected period.

4. How to ask

Reply to your Paddle receipt, or write to support@mykeepwise.com with the email you paid with. Refunds go back to the way you paid, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

5. Your rights

Nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under the consumer law where you live.
