Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

Summary

  • Keepwise has no user accounts and no server that stores your information.
  • Everything you enter or import, including bank statements, is processed and stored only on your own device.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information.

This summary is for convenience. The full policy below governs.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Keepwise, including the website at chillwillpill.github.io/keepwise, the installable web app and the Android app (together, the "Service"). In this policy, "Keepwise", "we", "us" and "our" refer to the operator of the Service, and "you" refers to any person who uses it.

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

2. How the Service works

Keepwise runs entirely on your device. Calculations, budgeting, subscription detection, statement analysis and the answers given by the "Ask Keepwise" feature are performed by code running on your device. The information you provide is saved in your browser's local storage or, in the Android app, in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us, and we have no means of viewing, accessing or recovering it.

3. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, the following information may be stored on your device:

  • income, pay dates, budget categories, savings goals and amounts set aside;
  • subscriptions, bills and expenses, including names, amounts, renewal dates and your notes on usage;
  • promotional codes and cheaper alternatives you save;
  • names of people you split costs with, and the amounts owed between you;
  • a summary of any bank statement you import, as described in section 4;
  • your settings, such as currency, theme and notification preferences.

This information remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by selecting "Start empty" in the Plan tab, by clearing the website data for Keepwise in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Bank statements

When you import a bank statement in CSV or PDF format, the file is opened and read on your device. The file is not uploaded to us or to any other party. Keepwise extracts the transactions needed to identify your income, recurring payments, subscriptions and spending categories, and saves the results of that analysis on your device. The original file is not retained by the Service.

To read PDF files, your device downloads a standard open source PDF reading library, pdf.js, from the cdnjs content delivery network the first time you import a PDF. Only the library is downloaded. The contents of your statement are never sent to cdnjs or to any other party.

We recommend that you import statements only on a device that you control and that is protected by a passcode or other screen lock.

5. Contacts and notifications

Contacts

If you choose to import contacts to split costs, you select the contacts yourself using your device's contact picker or a contact file. Only the names you select are saved, and only on your device. Keepwise never reads your full address book and never transmits contact details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are generated on your device. No push notification service, device token or identifier is used, and no information is sent to us to deliver them.

8. Cookies and local storage

Keepwise does not set cookies. It uses your browser's local storage and an offline cache solely to save your information on your device and to allow the app to work without a connection. This storage is strictly necessary to provide the features you request and is not used for tracking.

9. Legal bases for processing

To the extent that technical data is processed by the providers listed in section 6 in connection with delivering the Service, that processing relies on the legitimate interest in providing a secure and working service.

You have full control over your information because it is held only on your device. You may view, correct or delete it at any time using the app.

We do not hold personal information about you, and we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define those terms. If you contact us about your rights, we will respond within the time required by applicable law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Security

Because your information is stored on your device, its security depends on the security of that device. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or your browser profile may be able to view it. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device and browser up to date. We do not keep backups of your information, so it cannot be restored by us if your device is lost, reset or its storage is cleared.

The Service is intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly receive information from children.
