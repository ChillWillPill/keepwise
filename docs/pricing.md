# KeepWise Plus pricing (draft, 13 October 2026: waiting for final approval)

Prices are chosen by the card's country (web, Paddle) or the store country (Google Play), never by IP, so a VPN does not change them.
Each price is shown in local money.

| Tier | Countries (examples) | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia, Gulf | $15 (early offer $12 for 6 months, as in the app) | $180 ($140 first year, as in the app) |
| 2 | Latin America, Eastern Europe, Turkey, Malaysia, South Africa | $7.99 | $59.99 |
| 3 | Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Egypt, Nigeria, Philippines, Indonesia | $4.99 | $34.99 |

Plus includes bank connection through Plaid at no extra cost (KeepWise pays Plaid). Plus does not offer SimpleFIN, which is paid by the person to SimpleFIN; it stays an optional choice for free users.
